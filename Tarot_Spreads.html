How to Read

"The cards do not tell you what will happen. They show you what is present — and what is possible."

The Nature of Tarot Reading

Tarot is not fortune telling. It is a system of reflective mirrors — each card reflecting back an aspect of the situation, the energy present, the deeper pattern operating beneath the surface. The deck is a symbolic language encoding the full range of human experience across 78 cards. When you draw a card, you are not receiving a prediction. You are receiving a reflection — the universe showing you, through the symbolic language of the cards, what is actually present in your situation that your conscious mind may not be fully seeing.

The reader's job is not to translate the cards mechanically — "the Five of Swords means defeat" — but to enter into dialogue with the imagery, to notice what speaks, what resonates, what stirs something. The meaning that arises from genuine intuitive engagement with the card is always more accurate than the textbook definition.

Before Every Reading

Hold the deck in your hands for a moment. State your question or intention clearly — either aloud or mentally. Take three slow breaths. Shuffle until it feels complete, not to a predetermined number. Cut the deck once. The preparatory ritual is not superstition — it is the practice of shifting from beta (analytical) to alpha (receptive) brainwaves, which allows the intuitive faculty to operate more clearly. You are setting the frequency before the transmission.

Reversed Cards

Reversed (upside-down) cards can be read as the shadow or blocked expression of the card's energy, or as an inward-turned version of it — the energy present but operating internally rather than externally. When starting out, it is perfectly valid to read all cards upright and simply be aware of the full spectrum of each card's meaning including its shadow side. As your practice deepens, reversals add a useful layer of nuance.

The Celtic Cross

The Master Spread

The Celtic Cross

"The most comprehensive spread in tarot — ten cards, the full picture of any situation"

The Celtic Cross is the most widely used and most comprehensive spread in all of tarot. Ten cards, ten positions, each illuminating a different dimension of the situation. It takes 20-30 minutes to read properly and rewards that time with extraordinary depth and precision. Use it for major life questions, crossroads moments, or when you want the complete picture.

1The Heart of the Matter
The core of the situation. The central energy or challenge at the heart of your question.
2The Crossing Card
Placed across card 1. The immediate challenge, obstacle, or complicating energy. Not necessarily negative — sometimes it is the very thing that is helping, even if it feels like resistance.
3Above — The Ideal
The best possible outcome, the highest aspiration, or the conscious goal in this situation.
4Below — The Foundation
The unconscious influence, the deep root, the hidden foundation beneath the situation. Often reveals what is driving things at a level below conscious awareness.
5Past — What Passed
The recent past energy that is moving out of the situation. What has been, or is ending.
6Future — What Approaches
The near future — what is moving into the situation. An energy, person, or development approaching.
7Your Position
How you currently see yourself in relation to this situation. Your attitude, fears, or beliefs about it.
8External Influences
How others see this situation, or the environmental/external forces at play around you.
9Hopes and Fears
Often the most illuminating card. What you most hope for and most fear — which are frequently the same thing.
10The Outcome
The likely outcome if all current energies continue as they are. Not fixed — this is the trajectory, not the destination. It is showing you where you are heading so you can choose whether to continue or redirect.

Asking Good Questions

The Art of the Question

Questions

"The quality of the reading is determined by the quality of the question"

What Makes a Good Tarot Question

The best tarot questions are open, curious, and empowered. They come from genuine inquiry rather than fear or the desire to be told what you want to hear. They ask what, how, and what do I need to know — rather than will I or when will. The cards do not predict the future. They illuminate the present so clearly that the future becomes more navigable.

Powerful Question Formats
What do I need to know about [situation] right now?
What energy would most serve me to embody in [area of life]?
What am I not seeing about [situation] that would help me?
What is the most important thing for me to focus on this week / month / cycle?
What is this challenge here to teach me?
What would most support my growth right now?
What is the deeper pattern operating in [relationship / situation]?
What do I need to release to move forward?
What is my soul calling me toward right now?
