Morning Sequence — Before Your Phone
Step 1 · 1 minute
The First Breath
Before you open your eyes fully — three slow deep breaths. On each exhale, release whatever the night left behind. Then set your intention in one sentence.

Today I move with clarity. Today I am open. Today I create from strength.
Step 2 · 3 minutes
Gratitude Anchoring
Three specific things you are genuinely grateful for right now. Not generic. The coffee. A conversation. Something your body does. Genuine gratitude is one of the highest vibrational states available — starting your broadcast here changes the day's frequency.
Step 3 · 2 minutes
Today's Principle
Choose one Hermetic principle and hold it in mind for two minutes. Let it colour the day. → See the Principles tab.

Ask: Where will this principle show up in my day?
Step 4 · 2 minutes
The Intention
State clearly what you are calling in today. Positive. Present tense. Specific.

Not "I want to feel confident" — "I am moving through today with confidence and ease."

Speak it aloud if possible.
Step 5 · 5 minutes
Stillness
Sit. Do nothing. Receive. You have set your intention (masculine). Now let the deeper intelligence respond (feminine). Do not fill this time with thought. Simply observe what arises. The most important thing you need to know often arrives in this window.

Today's Planet
The Phone Rule
No phone for 30 minutes after waking. Every notification is someone else's mental world flooding yours — during the window when your subconscious is most receptive. Guard this window. It is the most valuable creative real estate in your day.
The Seven Hermetic Principles — Daily Application
Principle I
Mentalism
"The All is Mind. The Universe is Mental."
Everything you experience is mental in nature. Your thoughts are not private — they are broadcasts. What you hold consistently in mind, you create in experience.
Ask today: If my dominant thought right now is a creative instruction to the universe — what am I ordering?
Principle II
Correspondence
"As above, so below. As within, so without."
Your outer world is a mirror of your inner world. Patterns in your relationships, finances, and health correspond precisely to patterns operating inside you. Change the inner and the outer must follow.
Ask today: What is my outer world reflecting about my inner state right now?
Principle III
Vibration
"Nothing rests. Everything moves. Everything vibrates."
You are a transmitter and receiver. Your emotional state is a broadcast. Fear, gratitude, anger, love — all real frequencies. What you emit, you attract back. Use music, movement, breath, and beauty to shift your frequency deliberately.
Ask today: What frequency am I broadcasting right now — and what will that attract?
Principle IV
Polarity
"Everything is dual. Opposites are identical in nature, different in degree."
You are never stuck at one end of a spectrum. Fear and courage are the same energy at different positions. Love and hate are the same spectrum. You can move along any spectrum consciously — this is mental transmutation.
Ask today: Where am I standing on a spectrum that I could consciously move along?
Principle V
Rhythm
"Everything flows, out and in. The pendulum swings in everything."
Everything moves in cycles. High follows low. The depth of the swing one way is the measure of the return. When you are in a low phase, do not fight it — use it for rest, reflection, and planting. The return is coming.
Ask today: What phase of the cycle am I in — and what does this phase ask of me?
Principle VI
Cause & Effect
"Every cause has its effect. Chance is law not recognised."
Nothing is random. You are either a cause (acting from intention) or an effect (reacting to circumstances). The signs you see are effects — find the cause and find the message. Every deliberate action you take is a seed entering the causal chain.
Ask today: Am I acting as a cause or reacting as an effect right now?
Principle VII
Gender
"Gender is in everything. Everything has its masculine and feminine principles."
Creation requires both active will (masculine — plant the seed) and receptive trust (feminine — let it grow). After a period of strong action, enter receptive mode. After a period of waiting, act. Know which mode the moment requires.
Ask today: Does this moment call for directed action or patient trust?

Evening Sequence — The Hour Before Sleep
Step 1 · 5 minutes
The Review
Let the day pass through your mind like a film. Do not judge — observe. Notice where you acted from your highest self. Notice where you didn't. Notice where the principles showed up — what correspondences appeared, what caused what.
Step 2 · 3 minutes
The Release
Whatever carries a charge — frustration, worry, a conversation that didn't go right. Breathe in, name it, breathe out and let it go. You do not need to solve it tonight. Your subconscious will work on it. Release the emotional grip.
Step 3 · 2 minutes
Three Wins
Name three things that went well today — however small. The brain catalogues problems automatically. Consciously cataloguing wins retrains this bias and shifts your dominant broadcast toward appreciation before sleep.
Step 4 · 2 minutes
The Night Intention
Ask your deeper intelligence to work on something specific while you sleep. A problem needing solution. A decision needing clarity. State it simply, then release it. Do not try to think your way to an answer — plant the question and trust.
Step 5 · 10-15 minutes
The Tarot Card
Read tonight's card from the Major Arcana book. Let it be the last substantial thing your mind holds before sleep. The subconscious will work with it all night. Notice what dreams arise.
In Bed · Optional but powerful
Sleep Script + Binaural Beats
Headphones in. Theta binaural beats (4-8Hz) playing softly. Your sleep script recording over the top. Eyes closed. Let the words wash over you passively — do not actively listen. Drift. The subconscious has received its instructions.
