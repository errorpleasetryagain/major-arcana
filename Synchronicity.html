What Synchronicity Is

The Foundation

What It Is

"Coincidence is God's way of remaining anonymous — Albert Einstein"

The Word Itself

Carl Jung coined the term synchronicity in the 1950s to describe what he called meaningful coincidence — events that appear related not through cause and effect, but through meaning. Two things happening at the same time that seem to be connected — not because one caused the other, but because they share a significance that transcends mechanical causation.

Jung was careful to distinguish this from mere coincidence. Coincidence is random. Synchronicity carries felt meaning. The difference is internal — you know it when you encounter it. There is a quality of recognition, of this is significant, that accompanies genuine synchronicity. The feeling arrives before the interpretation does.

Adam's Example
The Fool card kept appearing — in passing, on social media, in a hotel — until you finally stopped and looked it up. That is synchronicity. Not because someone arranged it. Because your consciousness was ready to receive the message, and the universe responded to that readiness by presenting the symbol repeatedly until you noticed. The moment you asked the question — am I at a threshold? — the answer had already been appearing for weeks.
The Hermetic Foundation

Jung developed synchronicity as a modern psychological concept, but he was deeply versed in the Hermetic tradition and knew he was rediscovering something ancient. The Principle of Correspondence — as above, so below — is the metaphysical foundation of synchronicity. If the macrocosm and the microcosm mirror each other perfectly, then outer events and inner states are always in correspondence. What appears to be a coincidence in the outer world is always a reflection of something in the inner world — or a response to it.

The Principle of Cause and Effect adds another layer. What we call chance is simply causation operating at a level we cannot directly track. The chain of events that brought The Fool card to your attention multiple times was not random. It was a chain of causal correspondences operating through the mental fabric of reality — which, per Mentalism, is what everything is made of. Your readiness to receive the message was the cause. The repeated appearances were the effects. The fact that you could not trace the mechanical links between cause and effect does not make those links any less real.

"There are no coincidences. Only patterns that have not yet been recognised."

The Evidence

The Science

"Jung worked with physicist Wolfgang Pauli for twenty years to understand the physics of synchronicity"

Jung and Pauli

Carl Jung's collaboration with Nobel Prize winning physicist Wolfgang Pauli is one of the most extraordinary intellectual partnerships of the 20th century. Pauli was one of the founding fathers of quantum mechanics. He came to Jung suffering from severe psychological disturbance — and over two decades of collaboration, the two men built a bridge between depth psychology and quantum physics that neither field has fully crossed to this day.

Their central shared insight was that the quantum world and the psychological world operate by the same principles. In quantum mechanics, observation affects the observed — the act of measurement collapses the wave function, bringing one possibility out of infinite possibilities into specific reality. In Jungian psychology, bringing unconscious content into conscious awareness transforms it. Both processes involve the same fundamental dynamic: consciousness interacting with the field of possibility to create specific reality. Synchronicity is what this looks like from the inside.

The Quantum Field

Modern physics has established beyond reasonable doubt that at the quantum level, matter does not behave like solid objects following predictable paths. It behaves like information — like fields of probability that collapse into specific states when observed. The quantum vacuum — what was once called empty space — is now understood to be seething with energy, full of what physicists call virtual particles constantly popping in and out of existence. This is not metaphor. This is the current scientific consensus.

The Principle of Vibration from the Kybalion — everything vibrates, nothing rests — is a description of this quantum reality three thousand years before the physics existed to measure it. And if everything is vibration, then consciousness — which also vibrates — is not separate from the field of matter and events. It is participating in it, continuously, whether you are aware of it or not.

The Global Consciousness Project

Since 1998, the Global Consciousness Project at Princeton University has been running a continuous experiment with random number generators placed around the world. These generators should produce random output — but they consistently show significant deviations from randomness during events of global collective attention. The September 11 attacks. The death of Princess Diana. Major global sporting events. The generators began deviating from randomness hours before the events occurred.

The interpretation is controversial in mainstream science. But the data is robust and has been replicated across decades. Something about collective human attention affects the behavior of supposedly random physical systems. The universe responds to consciousness. This is synchronicity at a global scale.

"The day science begins to study non-physical phenomena, it will make more progress in one decade than in all the previous centuries of its existence — Nikola Tesla"

The Difference Between Signal and Noise

As your awareness opens, you will start to see potential synchronicities everywhere. This is natural — you are tuning a previously untuned instrument and the first stage is hypersensitivity. Over time, you will develop discernment. Here is how to begin distinguishing signal from noise:

The felt sense. Genuine synchronicity carries a quality of felt recognition — a sense of this is significant that arrives before you start interpreting. It is not the same as wishful thinking, which feels more like wanting something to mean what you want it to mean. The genuine signal has a quality of being received rather than constructed.

The question you were carrying. Synchronicities almost always arrive in response to something you are holding — consciously or unconsciously. They are answers to questions you have been asking, even if you have not articulated the question clearly. When a synchronicity arrives, ask yourself: what question was I carrying just before this appeared?

Repetition confirms. A single occurrence might be coincidence. Three occurrences in a short period from unrelated sources is almost always signal. The universe tends to be patient but persistent. The Fool appeared to you many times before you looked it up.

A Real Example of Signal vs Noise
You see a hawk once — interesting, perhaps nothing. You see a hawk the next day while contemplating a bold decision — slightly more notable. The following week you mention hawks in passing to a friend and they say they just read something about hawks as a symbol of clear vision and decisiveness — and you get goosebumps. That is signal. Three occurrences, different sources, bodily confirmation, directly relevant to a live question you are holding. The universe has underlined something three times. Pay attention.

The Practice

Working With It

The Practice

"You cannot force synchronicity. You can make yourself available to it."

1. Ask a Clear Question

Synchronicity responds to genuine inquiry. Before you can read the answers the universe is sending, you need to be asking a real question — not a vague wish, but a genuine, heartfelt inquiry. State it clearly, either mentally or in your journal. What do I need to know about this situation? What is the next right step? What am I not seeing? Am I on the right path?

The more honest and precise the question, the more useful the synchronicity that arrives. Vague questions generate vague signals. Real questions generate real answers.

2. Release the Question

After asking clearly, let go. Do not obsessively watch for the answer — that is the masculine principle gone into shadow, trying to force the feminine receptive process. Plant the question and trust. The answer will come in the right form at the right time. Your job after asking is simply to remain present and attentive, not to scan anxiously for signals.

This is the Principle of Gender in practice. Ask with clear intention — masculine. Trust and receive — feminine. The synchronicity arrives in the space between.

3. Stay Present

Most synchronicities are missed not because they do not occur but because we are not present enough to notice them. The mind that is lost in past or future — anxious, planning, ruminating — cannot register the subtle signal of the present moment. Presence is the prerequisite for synchronicity awareness. This is why meditation, breathwork, and any practice that brings you fully into the now directly increases your experience of meaningful coincidence. It is not magical. You are simply more available.

4. Follow the Thread

When a synchronicity arrives, follow it. Not compulsively, not abandoning all discernment — but with genuine curiosity and willingness. A book keeps appearing? Read it. A person's name keeps coming up? Reach out. A place calls to you repeatedly? Go there. The synchronicity is rarely the complete message. It is the first thread of a larger pattern. Pulling the thread reveals more.

This is how The Fool operates. He does not require the full picture before he steps. He notices the signal — the sun, the horizon, the step that wants to be taken — and he moves. The next step reveals the one after it.

5. Say Thank You

When you notice a synchronicity, acknowledge it. Not with superstition but with genuine gratitude — the same gratitude you would feel toward a friend who gave you exactly the information you needed at exactly the right moment. Because that is what is happening. The universe, which is conscious and communicative, has sent you something precisely calibrated to your situation. Thank you is the right response. It also deepens the conversation — gratitude is one of the highest vibrational states, and a high vibration receives more clearly.

The Synchronicity Journal

The Record

The Journal

"Writing a synchronicity down is the act of receiving it fully"

Why Keep a Record

The synchronicity journal is one of the most powerful practices available to anyone on this path. For three reasons. First — writing it down completes the act of reception. Until you record a synchronicity it remains in the half-noticed peripheral zone of experience. Writing it brings it fully into conscious awareness, which deepens its effect and sends a clear signal back to the universe that you received the message.

Second — patterns only become visible over time. A single synchronicity is interesting. Twenty synchronicities recorded over three months reveal themes, recurring symbols, and a clear direction that you simply cannot perceive without the record. The journal is how you read the larger conversation rather than just individual sentences of it.

Third — it builds trust. When you look back at your journal after several months and see how consistently and precisely the universe has been communicating with you, your trust in the process deepens fundamentally. This trust then makes you more available, which generates more synchronicity, which deepens trust further. A positive spiral with no ceiling.

The Five Questions for Each Entry
What happened exactly? Describe the synchronicity in plain terms — what occurred, when, and in what context.
What was I thinking, feeling, or questioning just before it appeared?
What does this mean to me personally — not what it might mean in general, but what my gut response says right now?
Which Hermetic principle is this demonstrating? Correspondence? Cause and Effect? Vibration? Mentalism?
What is the most generous and useful interpretation — if this synchronicity is helping me, what is it helping me see or do?
Monthly Review

At the end of each month, read back through your journal entries and look for patterns. What symbols keep recurring? What areas of your life does the universe seem most interested in communicating about? What questions have been answered? What new questions have opened? This monthly review is how the individual synchronicities combine into a coherent ongoing conversation. It is one of the most extraordinary experiences available to a conscious human being — reading the record of a months-long dialogue with the intelligence that underlies all of reality.

"You have always been in conversation with the universe. The journal is simply where you start writing down your side of it."

A Note on Discernment

As with all of this work, genuine discernment matters. Not everything is a sign. Not every coincidence is meaningful. The practice of recording synchronicities sharpens your discernment over time — you will notice the difference between the genuine felt recognition of signal and the wishful thinking of someone who wants everything to mean something. Both exist. The practice teaches you to tell them apart.

The test is always: does acting on this interpretation lead to greater clarity, greater openness, greater alignment with what feels genuinely true? Or does it lead to confusion, magical thinking, or avoidance of real responsibility? Genuine synchronicity always points toward more consciousness, more presence, more engagement with reality. Never toward less.
