What They Are

Chakra means wheel or disc in Sanskrit — a spinning vortex of energy within the human subtle body. The system describes seven primary energy centres arranged along the spine from the base to the crown of the head, each governing a specific domain of physical, emotional, mental, and spiritual experience. The system originates in ancient Vedic and Tantric texts of India, dating back at least four thousand years. But extraordinarily similar systems appear in ancient Egyptian, Kabbalistic, Hermetic, and Sufi traditions — suggesting not cultural borrowing but independent discovery of the same actual architecture of human consciousness.

In modern terms, the seven chakras correspond precisely to the seven major nerve plexuses of the autonomic nervous system, the seven major endocrine glands, and seven distinct regions of the brain associated with different types of awareness and behaviour. The ancient map and the modern map describe the same territory in different languages.

The Hermetic Parallel

Seven chakras. Seven Hermetic principles. Seven planets of the ancient world. Seven notes in the musical scale. Seven colours of the spectrum. Seven days of the week. The number seven appears at the intersection of the cosmic and the human across virtually every tradition in history. This is Correspondence operating at the structural level — the same pattern recurring at every scale of reality because it is describing something fundamental about the architecture of consciousness itself.

First Chakra
Muladhara — Root
Base of Spine · Earth · Survival
Location
Base of spine, perineum
Element
Earth · Dense matter
Endocrine
Adrenal glands
Hermetic
Principle of Vibration at its densest
The root chakra governs survival, safety, physical existence, and the fundamental sense of belonging to the earth. When balanced: groundedness, financial stability, physical vitality, trust in life's basic provision. When blocked: chronic fear, financial anxiety, disconnection from the body, the feeling that the world is fundamentally unsafe. The adrenal glands — which govern the fight-or-flight stress response — sit at this chakra's physical location. Most people in the modern world have a chronically overactivated root chakra from continuous low-level stress. Grounding practices, time in nature, physical exercise, and the Hermetic practice of trusting the rhythm all work directly on this centre.

Below Navel · Water · Creation
Location
Below the navel
Element
Water · Flow · Feeling
Endocrine

Hermetic
Principle of Gender — creative force
The sacral chakra governs creativity, sexuality, pleasure, emotion, and the flow of life force energy. It is the seat of the creative impulse in its most primal form — the urge to make things, to connect, to feel, to enjoy existence. When balanced: creative flow, healthy relationships, emotional intelligence, pleasure without guilt. When blocked: creative blocks, emotional numbness or overwhelm, unhealthy relationship to sexuality, inability to enjoy life. The Empress card in Tarot corresponds precisely to a healthy, open sacral chakra. The sacral and root together form the foundation of the Hermetic Principle of Gender in the body.

Third Chakra
Manipura — Solar Plexus
Above Navel · Fire · Will
Location
Above the navel, stomach
Element
Fire · Transformation
Endocrine
Pancreas, adrenals
Hermetic
Cause and Effect — personal power
The solar plexus is the seat of personal power, will, self-esteem, and identity. Manipura means city of jewels. It is the fire centre — the place where raw energy is transformed into directed action. The gut feeling, the gut instinct, the gut punch of anxiety — all of these are the solar plexus communicating. When balanced: strong self-worth, clear boundaries, decisive action, healthy ego. When blocked: people-pleasing, inability to say no, chronic self-doubt, or its opposite — bullying, need for control, ego inflation. The Magician card corresponds to a fully activated solar plexus working in harmony with the higher centres. This is the body's centre of the Hermetic principle of Cause and Effect — the place where you choose to be a cause rather than an effect.

Tiphareth on the Tree of Life
The heart chakra is the bridge between the three lower chakras (earth, survival, personal power) and the three higher chakras (expression, perception, transcendence). It is the integrating centre — where the human and the divine meet. Anahata means unstruck — the sound that exists before anything is struck, the silence that underlies all sound. When balanced: unconditional love, compassion, genuine connection, the ability to give and receive freely. When blocked: grief, isolation, inability to trust, conditional love. The HeartMath Institute has demonstrated that the electromagnetic field generated by the heart is sixty times stronger than that of the brain — and that states of love and gratitude create coherent, high-frequency fields that literally affect the space around you. This is the Principle of Vibration at its most potent human expression.

Location

The Word — vibration made audible
The throat chakra governs expression, communication, authenticity, and the power of the spoken word. In Hermetic tradition, the Word — the Logos — is the creative principle through which the divine brings reality into being. In Genesis, God speaks and it is so. In the Emerald Tablet, all things are created by the will and word of the One. The throat is where inner reality becomes outer expression — where the private becomes public, where the invisible becomes audible. When balanced: clear honest expression, the ability to speak your truth, the power to name things accurately. When blocked: inability to speak up, chronic throat issues, swallowing feelings, or its opposite — compulsive talking to avoid real communication. Reading your sleep script aloud activates this chakra deliberately — using the power of sound to imprint the subconscious.

The All-Seeing Eye — direct perception
The third eye chakra governs intuition, perception, imagination, and the direct knowing that transcends ordinary sensory experience. Its physical correspondence is the pineal gland — a tiny pine-cone shaped gland in the centre of the brain that Descartes called the seat of the soul. The pineal produces melatonin (governing sleep and the biological clock) and DMT (the most potent psychedelic molecule produced by the human body, released in high concentrations during birth, death, and deep dreaming). It is the most light-sensitive organ in the body and is directly connected to the visual cortex. When balanced: strong intuition, clear inner vision, the ability to perceive patterns and meaning, active synchronicity awareness. When blocked: overthinking, inability to trust intuition, disconnection from inner guidance. The Eye of Horus — the symbol we studied in the Masonic images — is a precise anatomical diagram of the third eye and its surrounding brain structures.

The master gland of the body at the point of connection with the master intelligence of the universe.

Daily Chakra Practice

Working With The System

The Practice

"You do not need to balance all seven every day. You need to know which one is calling for attention."

The Daily Scan

Each morning as part of your practice, take two minutes to scan your body from base to crown. Move your attention slowly upward through each chakra location — base of spine, below navel, solar plexus, heart, throat, between the brows, top of the head. Notice where you feel open and flowing versus where you feel contracted, heavy, numb, or absent. This is not analysis — it is listening. The body will tell you what needs attention today.

The Body Question

When you notice a blockage or restriction in a chakra location, ask one question: What is this centre trying to tell me? The root speaking in anxiety — what threat does it perceive? The solar plexus contracted — whose boundaries are being crossed? The throat tight — what truth needs to be spoken? The body speaks in sensation. Sensation is information. Learn to translate it.

Simple Activations

Root — Walk barefoot on earth or grass. Physical exercise. Eat whole, grounding foods. Breathe deeply into the base of the body.

Sacral — Create something. Move freely. Allow pleasure without guilt. Dance, cook, make music, make anything.

Solar Plexus — Take one decisive action you have been avoiding. Hold a boundary. Do something that requires courage. Breathe into the stomach area.

Heart — Genuine gratitude practice. Acts of giving without expectation. Time with people you love. Breathe into the chest. Place a hand on your heart.

Throat — Speak one truth you have been swallowing. Sing, even alone. Read aloud. Record your sleep script. Express something honestly.

Third Eye — Sit in silence for ten minutes without agenda. Journal about a dream or synchronicity. Meditate with eyes closed and attention between the brows.

Crown — Spend time in genuine awe — in nature, with music, in meditation. Read something that expands your sense of what is possible. Let go of the need to understand and simply be.
