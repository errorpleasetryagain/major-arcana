# ✦ The Major Arcana — A Deep Study

> *One card. One night. One layer deeper.*

A personal grimoire built card by card — a deep, immersive study of the 22 Major Arcana cards of the Tarot. Not fortune-telling. A map of the human soul, drawn by the ancients, encoded in symbol, and brought to life as a living interactive book.

---

## ☽ What This Is

This is a single-page interactive HTML book designed to be read on any device — particularly iPad, in bed, before sleep. One card a night. Each card gets its own hand-illustrated SVG artwork, deep esoteric commentary, Kabbalistic and Hermetic layers, Egyptian connections, numerology, shadow readings, and a closing meditation.

The book grows over time. Every card added is another night of the journey.

## ✦ What Each Card Contains

- **Hand-illustrated SVG card** — original artwork drawn in code, faithful to the Rider-Waite symbolism
- **Keyword grid** — element, Hebrew letter, Kabbalistic path, numerology
- **Deep esoteric commentary** — Hermetic, Kabbalistic, Egyptian, and Jungian layers
- **Pull quotes** — the single most important idea from each card
- **Upright / Reversed table** — practical reading guide
- **Closing meditation** — the card's essential message in plain language

---

## ✦ The Design

- Deep night sky with animated starfield
- Gold typography on dark — designed for night reading
- Scroll-reveal animations — content fades in as you read
- Sticky card label — always know where you are
- Gold progress bar — tracks how far through the book you are
- Fully responsive — iPad, iPhone, desktop
- **No external dependencies** — works completely offline once loaded

---

## ☽ The Philosophy

This is not a beginner's guide. It goes deep — into the Kabbalistic Tree of Life, Hermetic philosophy, ancient Egyptian mystery schools, Jungian archetypes, and sacred geometry. Each card is treated as a complete teaching in itself, and together they tell a single story: the Fool's Journey, which is the story of every human soul moving through incarnation toward wholeness.

> *"The Fool is not the beginning of the story. The Fool is what exists before stories are possible."*
